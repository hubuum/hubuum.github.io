Each complete scrolling study gives data ownership, schema choices, and relationships their own space. The same CMDB can hold data whose authority lives here or elsewhere, with structured and free-form content side by side.

Confluence is the calmest direction. Orbit makes the strongest statement about the hub. Fabric gives the model an architectural, editorial character. Defined and open record motifs carry the new focus into every illustration.

The illustrations describe the product’s ideas. The source and schema labels in the example model explain a possible setup, rather than showing an existing graph interface or automatic source management.
